Abstract

Why multi-modal time series?

Time-series data is fundamental to prediction and decision-making, and it increasingly appears together with text, images, event logs, reports, and other contextual signals. These additional modalities can improve forecasting and support richer forms of reasoning, but they also introduce challenges in temporal alignment, heterogeneous representations, missing information, evaluation, and scale.

This tutorial presents recent advances in multi-modal time-series learning across forecasting, reasoning, and question answering. We connect deep forecasting architectures, pretrained language and vision-language models, and time-series foundation models, while emphasizing practical modelling choices and evaluation.

After the tutorial

Participants will be able to

  • Formalise multimodal time-series tasks and their central challenges.
  • Select fusion strategies and sequence backbones for paired time series and text.
  • Explain how language models and foundation models integrate temporal and textual signals.
  • Adapt the provided Colab notebooks to their own research problems.
Audience. Ph.D. students, researchers, and practitioners with an intermediate ML background and basic PyTorch familiarity.
